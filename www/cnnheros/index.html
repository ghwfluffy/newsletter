A nomination story told through evidence

This page is organized around the requested CNN Heroes support photos. The sequence begins with Jane Fuller and the landscape SPJ serves, then moves through housing, construction, worship and community buildings, children and youth programs, medical care, clothing, food, and winter wood distribution.

The strongest through-line is continuity: SPJ is not a single project or trip. The photos show a network of help built over time, with Jane present in the field and families using the spaces and services after they are created.

Nominee Jane Fuller, serving families through Siguiendo los Pasos de Jesus.
Place Colonias of Ciudad Juarez, Mexico, where isolation, dirt roads, and informal housing shape daily life.
Work shown Food, clothing, housing, education support, medical care, community spaces, transportation, and winter heating wood.
